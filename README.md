## Oracles & Data
- [XDChain Oracle](https://xdcoracle.ripitlabs.com) — **47 live, signed on-chain price & real-world-asset feeds** on XDC. Chainlink `AggregatorV3`-compatible + public REST API. Crypto, FX, commodities, rates (Fed Funds, SOFR, UST curve), RWA (CGO), indices (VIX). Contract [`0x17e46C96…`](https://explorer.xinfin.network/address/0x17e46C9697c99a996a4230761e73a9452E7D603E).

## Developer Tools & SDKs
- [xdchain-oracle](https://github.com/ripitlabs-dev/xdchain-oracle) — JS/TS client for the XDChain Oracle (`npm i xdchain-oracle`).
- [XDC-connect](https://github.com/XinFinOrg) — XinFin org repositories & tooling.
- Standard EVM tooling works on XDC: **ethers.js**, **web3.js**, **Hardhat**, **Foundry** (point them at the XDC RPC, chain ID 50).

## RWA & Trade Finance
- [XDChain suite](https://xdcchain.ripitlabs.com) by Rip It Labs — enterprise ERPs on XDC for air, marine, rail, road, port, warehouse freight + trade finance, energy, agriculture & carbon, each anchoring records on-chain.
- [Comtech Gold (CGO)](https://comtechgold.com) — gold-backed RWA token on XDC.
- [Tradeteq](https://www.tradeteq.com) — trade-finance-as-an-asset-class, on XDC.
